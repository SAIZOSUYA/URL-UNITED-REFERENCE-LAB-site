Laboratory Departments & High-Tech Analyzers

US FDA Approved

Roche Cobas e411 ECL Analyzer

Compact, fully automated immunoassay analyzer using patented ElectroChemiLuminescence (ECL) technology for superior analytical sensitivity.

  • Hormones: TSH, FT3, FT4, Prolactin, Insulin, LH, FSH, AMH, PTH
  • Cardiac Risk: Troponin T, Troponin-hs, CK-MB, Myoglobin
  • Tumor Markers: PSA, CA-125, CA 19.9, CA-21.1, HE4, AFP, CEA
CE Marked & ISO 13485

Cormay Accent M320 Chemistry

Fully automated, computer-controlled clinical chemistry analyzer complying with European Union IVDR regulations for high-throughput biochemistry.

  • Liver/Hepatic: ALT, AST, ALP, Bilirubin (Total/Direct), GGT, Albumin
  • Renal/Kidney: Creatinine (Enzymatic/Jaffe), Urea, Uric Acid, Microalbumin
  • Diabetic & Cardiac: Glucose, HbA1c Direct, Fructosamine, Lactate, CK, LDH
Gold Standard

HPLC Diabetes & Thalassemia System

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography offering gold-standard precision for diabetes monitoring and hemoglobinopathy screening.

  • HbA1c Monitoring: Gold standard for diabetes tracking
  • Genetic Screening: Thalassemia & Hemoglobin Variant analysis
  • High Speed: Direct quantitative measurements without repeats

KDE Tissue Processing & Embedding

State-of-the-art tissue embedding system for surgical pathology, biopsy examination, and cancer margin evaluation.

  • LBC Cytology: Clean cervical cancer screening & HPV DNA testing
Biosafety Certified

Microbiology & Culture Unit

Comprehensive pathogen testing and automated sensitivity analysis in a controlled, biosafety-certified environment.

  • Microscopy: Gram staining, AFB tuberculosis testing & Fungal studies
  • Infectious Disease: HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Dengue, Scrub Typhus
Quality Controlled Workflow

Our 5-Step Diagnostic Process

From seamless sample collection to consultant verification, our process guarantees precision and rapid turnaround.

1
STEP 01 • WALK-IN OR HOME

Sample Collection & Registration

Walk-in at any of our 24 service outlets or request home sample collection. Samples are barcoded instantly for zero error tracking.

2
STEP 02 • SAFETY & CHECK

Pre-Analytical Safety Check

Barcoded samples undergo rigorous centrifugation, plasma separation, and volume checks following international biosafety protocols.

3
STEP 03 • HIGH-TECH ANALYZER

US FDA Automated Testing

Samples are processed using US FDA-approved analyzers (Roche Cobas e411, Cormay Accent M320, HPLC) with automatic calibration.

4
STEP 04 • EXPERT VERIFY

Pathologist & Biochemist Verification

All test outputs are evaluated by our expert consultant Pathologists, Microbiologists, and Biochemists prior to report release.

5
STEP 05 • INSTANT REPORT

Instant Digital Report Delivery

Receive your official QR-verified PDF report via SMS, Email, or Online Web Portal with doctor consultation guidance.
