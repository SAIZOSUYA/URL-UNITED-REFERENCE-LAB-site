About URL Group

United Reference Laboratory Pvt. Ltd.

Established on 23rd Mangsir 2070 (2013) in Pokhara, United Reference Laboratory (URL) is a trusted diagnostic leader in Nepal. Equipped with a strong network of 24 outlets across Gandaki Province (Kaski, Tanahun, Lamjung, Syangja, Nawalpur, Baglung), URL brings advanced reference-level diagnostics closer to communities.

Driven by cutting-edge technology, stringent ISO 15189 / NABL quality standards, and a dedicated team of over 180 professionals and 68 shareholders, URL fulfills the vital need for specialized, highly sensitive diagnostic testing locally—eliminating the need to send samples abroad.

Our Vision

To be Nepal’s most reliable, technology-driven, and internationally accredited reference laboratory, recognized for precision diagnostics, scientific excellence, and patient-centered services.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, timely, and evidence-based diagnostic services aligned with global standards, robust quality control (IQC/EQAS), continuous innovation, and ethical governance.

Roche Cobas e411 Analyzer

Compact, fully automated immunoassay analyzer using patented ElectroChemiLuminescence (ECL) technology for superior analytical sensitivity.

  • Cardiac Risk Markers: Troponin T, Troponin-hs, CK-MB, Myoglobin
  • Tumor Markers: Prostate PSA, CA-125, CA 19.9, CA-21.1, HE4, AFP, CEA
  • Specialty Tests: Vitamin B12, Vitamin D3, Tacrolimus levels
CE Marked & ISO 13485

Cormay Accent M320 Analyzer

Fully automated, computer-controlled clinical chemistry analyzer complying with European Union IVDR regulations.

  • Liver/Hepatic: ALT, AST, ALP, Bilirubin (Total/Direct), GGT, Albumin
  • Renal/Kidney: Creatinine (Enzymatic/Jaffe), Urea, Uric Acid, Microalbumin
  • Diabetic & Cardiac: Glucose, HbA1c Direct, Fructosamine, Lactate, CK, LDH
  • Electrolytes & Proteins: Na+, K+, Cl-, Calcium, ASO, RF, Ferritin, CRP
Gold Standard

HPLC Diabetes & Thalassemia System

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography offering superior precision and speed for critical health markers.

  • Genetic Screening: Screening for genetic blood disorders like Thalassemia
  • High Speed & Accuracy: Direct quantitative measurements without repeats

Patient Education

7 Smart Tips to Consider in Diagnostic Lab Testing

1

Verify NPHL registration and category accreditation.

2

Choose ISO 9001:2015 certified and trusted laboratories.

3

Confirm tests are performed locally within Nepal for quick TAT.

4

Prefer laboratories with US FDA-approved automated systems.

5

Ensure reports are reviewed and signed by qualified specialists.

6

Avoid unrealistically low-cost test offers compromising quality.

7

Check reviews, hygiene, and sample collection standards.
